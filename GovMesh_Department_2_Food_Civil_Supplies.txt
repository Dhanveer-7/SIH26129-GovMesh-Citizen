GOVMESH – SIH26129
DEPARTMENT 2 – FOOD, CIVIL SUPPLIES & CONSUMER PROTECTION
SIMULATED DEPARTMENT SYSTEM – MVP REQUIREMENTS
============================================================

PURPOSE
-------
Department 2 represents the Food, Civil Supplies & Consumer Protection
Department in the GovMesh demonstration.

Its primary purpose is to prove that GovMesh can integrate a department
using a DIFFERENT technology/protocol from Department 1.

Recommended simulated integration:
SOAP + XML

This creates a visible interoperability challenge:
Department 1 = REST/JSON
Department 2 = SOAP/XML

============================================================
5. SOAP/XML INTEGRATION
============================================================

Department 2 should deliberately expose a SOAP service.

Example operation:

UpdateRationAddress

Request:

<UpdateRationAddress>
    <ApplicationId>GM-2026-000124</ApplicationId>
    <CitizenName>XXXXX</CitizenName>
    <Address>XXXXX</Address>
    <RevenueVerified>true</RevenueVerified>
    <ConsentId>CONSENT-00124</ConsentId>
</UpdateRationAddress>

Response:

<UpdateRationAddressResponse>
    <ApplicationId>GM-2026-000124</ApplicationId>
    <Status>SUCCESS</Status>
</UpdateRationAddressResponse>

GovMesh converts its canonical JSON representation into XML/SOAP.

12. IDEMPOTENCY
============================================================

Important technical feature.

If GovMesh retries the same request, Department 2 must not
perform the same update twice.

Use:
- Application ID
- Request ID
- Idempotency key

Example:

Request:
GM-2026-000124

If already processed:
Return previous result instead of creating another update.

============================================================
20. SUCCESS CRITERIA
============================================================

The evaluator should see:

1. Department 2 is technologically different from Department 1.
2. GovMesh receives standardized internal data.
3. GovMesh transforms the data.
4. SOAP/XML request is generated.
5. Department validates consent.
6. Department processes the request.
7. SOAP response is transformed back.
8. Citizen receives one unified status.
9. Retry does not duplicate the update.

FINAL MESSAGE:
"GovMesh enables interoperability even when departments use
different protocols and data models."
