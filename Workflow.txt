1. What query should you give?

For your MVP, I strongly recommend using one realistic query that genuinely requires all three departments.

Best demo query

"I have shifted to a new address. I want to update my address in my government records, including my Revenue records, ration card records, and relevant local rural government records."

This is much better than simply typing:

"Change my address."

Because your first query explicitly demonstrates the need for three different government systems.

3. LLM understands the query

The LLM should not call Department 1, 2 or 3.

Instead, it produces a structured intent.

For example:

{
  "intent": "ADDRESS_CHANGE",
  "entities": {
    "service": "government_record_address_update"
  },
  "suggested_services": [
    "REVENUE_ADDRESS_UPDATE",
    "RATION_ADDRESS_UPDATE",
    "RURAL_RECORD_UPDATE"
  ]
}

The LLM essentially says:

"This citizen wants an address change. These are the government services that may be involved."

9. Department 3 connection

Then GovMesh triggers Department 3.

But this system is deliberately designed as a legacy system.

So instead of REST:

🎯 So what exact query should your team use?

For your main SIH demo, use:

"I have shifted to a new address. I want to update my address in my government records, including my Revenue records, ration card records, and relevant local rural government records."

Then demonstrate these three mappings:
Citizen request	GovMesh service	Department	Protocol
Update Revenue record	REVENUE_ADDRESS_UPDATE	Revenue & Forest	REST/JSON
Update ration record	RATION_ADDRESS_UPDATE	Food & Civil Supplies	SOAP/XML
Update local rural record	RURAL_RECORD_UPDATE	Rural Development	CSV/SFTP

This is much stronger than making the LLM directly "connect" to three departments.

Your core innovation should be:

The LLM understands the citizen's intent; GovMesh determines the approved service workflow; the interoperability layer translates and routes data to heterogeneous departmental systems; and the citizen receives one unified status.
